Real products, prices, stock and units from FUTURE DISPO. The checkout overlay was done separately; this is the screen behind it, where the cashier actually spends the shift.

  • Density. Your screenshot shows two products in a pane that fits nine. A tighter row puts the shelf on screen instead of behind a scroll.
  • A real bug this surfaced: Carry Bag and CARRY BAG are two separate pills over 16 products. Picking one hides the other's stock, and neither pill tells you it is missing half. The sample folds them case-insensitively and puts a count on each pill.
  • Unit and tax on the row. Prices here are per KG, PCS and PACK — 16*20 White cover is ₹125 a kilo. Two products are also genuinely 0% GST; the cashier cannot see that today.
  • Stock is a state, not a number. Low is amber, zero is greyed and cannot be added. 13×16 BLACK is down to 6 KG.
  • Three products have no category at all and so appear under no pill — reachable only through All or by knowing the name. The sample gives them an Uncategorised pill rather than leaving them invisible.
