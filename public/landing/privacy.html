Privacy Policy.

How we protect your business ledgers and customer data — compliant with modern data-protection standards.

Last modified: May 26, 2026 · Version 2.3

1. Data Hosting & In-Region Storage

All bookledger accounting ledgers, client records, and transactional databases are hosted securely on Supabase backend infrastructure. To ensure legal compliance and fast loading, our database instances are physically located in local availability regions (AWS Mumbai).

2. Absolute Zero-Selling Commitment

We maintain a strict stance on client confidentiality: we do not sell, lease, or monetize your accounting or customer data to third parties. Your client list and margin structures are yours alone.

3. Security & Data Protection Standards

bookledger maintains technical and organizational safeguards for sensitive personal data, including AES-256 database encryption and continuous backups.

4. Financial & GST Data Retention

We retain historical ledger files while your subscription remains active. Should your account become dormant, we follow structured regulatory guidelines to secure records while enabling you to access historical GSTR spreadsheets.

5. The Right to Deletion

You retain absolute ownership over your database. You may request deletion of your complete administrative console and tenant data at any time. Upon processing, our systems securely purge all tenant storage allocations.
