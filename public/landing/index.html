Frequently Asked Questions

How secure is my business data?

All business entries are stored on encrypted-at-rest Postgres (Supabase). Your operational data is backed up daily by our hosting provider.

Can I export my reports and invoices?

Yes. You can export complete day books, client credit ledgers, stock lists, and all 28 analytical reports into clean Excel or CSV spreadsheets at any time.

Is LedgrPro GST-ready?

Absolutely. Set tax percentages at item creation, and LedgrPro handles CGST, SGST, and IGST tagging on every transaction. Export file spreadsheets align perfectly with active government tax filing setups.

Does the POS terminal work offline?

Yes. The billing checkout runs directly within local browser caches. You can continue scanning products and issuing receipts without an active internet connection; all transactions sync once connection re-establishes.

What happens after the 60-day free trial?

No credit card is required to try LedgrPro. After your 60-day free trial, you can choose to subscribe to either the Starter or Pro tier, or cancel anytime with no penalties or lock-ins.
