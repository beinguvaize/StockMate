POS checkout — new sale window sample — nothing built yet

Real products and prices from FUTURE DISPO. The current screen works, but it is laid out for a mouse: Amount Received is a small optional field and Change due is a small chip beneath it. At a counter those are the two things that matter most.

  • One number dominates, and it changes as you go. Before tendering it is AMOUNT DUE; the moment cash is entered the same block turns green and becomes CHANGE TO RETURN. The cashier never hunts for the figure they act on.
  • Tender presets and a keypad. Cash is counted in notes, so the presets are notes — exact, and the next ₹500/₹1000/₹2000 up. Typing on a keyboard is not how a busy counter works.
  • Split payment — ₹1,000 by UPI and the rest cash is normal here and is impossible on the current screen; it takes one payment method per sale.
  • Keyboard first. F1–F4 pick the method, Enter completes. A trained cashier should never need the mouse.
