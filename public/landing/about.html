About

Software that respects how Indian shops actually run.

Patchy internet, GST deadlines, cash drawers, van routes, udhaar. We built bookledger for that reality — offline-first, GST-correct, and fast enough to use at the counter.

Most billing apps assume a perfect connection and a desktop. Indian retail doesn't work like that. A sale happens whether or not the network is up. Stock moves across stores and vehicles. Dues pile up. Returns need the original rate. GST has to tie out at month-end.

bookledger is one ledger for all of it — billing, POS, inventory, expenses, receivables, payroll and reporting — that works the same on web, desktop and a free mobile app. Everything runs locally and syncs when you reconnect, so the counter never waits.

No bloat, no per-feature upsells, no surprise lock-ins. Just a quiet, fast back office that keeps your books honest.
