Changelog

What shipped, and when.

Every release that changed something you can see. Bug fixes and performance work go out continuously and are not listed here.

v1.8.0

13 May 2026

Multi-location stock and faster client search

  • — Multi-location inventory. Track the same item across separate shopfronts and godowns, natively.
  • — Client search at the till. A type-to-search box on the POS, so a regular is found while they are still talking.
  • — B2B / B2C classification. Mark a client's accounting status and map them to a wholesale price tier.
v1.7.2

28 April 2026

State GST auto-fill and GSTR-1 export

  • — GST auto-fill. State codes are looked up from the GSTIN on the client form, which is where SGST/IGST mix-ups start.
  • — GSTR-1 export. Month-end filing exports straight out of the books.
v1.6.0

12 April 2026

Offline billing and the mobile app

  • — Offline-first billing. Bills are taken and held on the device when the line drops, and sync when it returns.
  • — Android app. The full till in your pocket, free with every plan.
