Questions, answered.

Does it really work offline?

add

Yes. Sales, billing and stock all run fully offline. An outbox queues every change and syncs automatically when you reconnect — nothing is lost.

Is it GST compliant?

add

CGST/SGST/IGST, HSN, inclusive/exclusive tax, and GSTR-1/3B exports are built in. Invoices follow Indian GST formatting.

Can I use it on my phone?

add

Yes — a free iOS & Android app mirrors the web app, including offline billing and print-perfect invoices.
