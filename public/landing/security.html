Trust

Platform Security.

How we keep your business data private, safe and always recoverable.

Updated May 2026

1. Your data stays yours

Every business on bookledger is kept completely separate. Your sales, stock and customer records are walled off from every other account — there is no way for another business to see or reach your data.

2. Secure sign-in

  • Passwords stay hidden: we never store your password in a form anyone can read.
  • Encrypted connection: everything you send and receive is scrambled end to end, so it can't be read in transit.
  • Single sign-on: larger teams can sign in with their company login.

3. Daily backups

Your data is backed up automatically every day. If anything ever goes wrong, we can restore your records from a recent safe copy.

4. Activity history

On the Enterprise plan, bookledger keeps a clear, unchangeable history of who did what — who raised an invoice, adjusted stock or changed a price, with the exact date and time.
